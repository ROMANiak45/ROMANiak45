## 🚀 About Me

My path into iOS wasn't typical: I studied medicine, then spent six years as CEO of a company, before moving into iOS development in 2019. That leadership time still shapes how I work. I take ownership of what I ship, I think about the product and not only the code, and I communicate clearly with teams and stakeholders.

- 🏗️ I like code that the next developer enjoys opening.
- 📦 I enjoy both sides of the job: shipping full apps and building SDKs other developers rely on.
- 🌱 I'm always exploring SwiftUI, Combine and async/await.
- 🗣️ I speak Romanian, English and Russian.
- 💬 Ask me about iOS architecture, SDK design, or moving into tech from another field.
